Hi, I'm Adam. I run **SCENE-FLIX**, a fan site for RESCENE.

<br>

### [SCENE-FLIX](https://github.com/Adam-yam/SCENE-FLIX)

A place to find RESCENE videos from different channels.  
It brings together music shows, original content, livestreams and fan channels, along with schedules, news and music charts.

[Visit the site ↗](https://adam-yam.github.io/SCENE-FLIX/)
